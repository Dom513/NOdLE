How to Play NOdLE

Trace along the lines to draw the five numbers that complete the sum!

You can tap a number in the sum to place it back on the board if you've made a mistake.

Tap lines to unhighlight them or click "Clear" to unhighlight them all.

Click the question mark to get a hint.

Try some practice puzzles if you want more help identifying what different versions of the numbers can look like.
